AI marketing automation starts with **deciding what to automate**, not with picking tools. Get the order wrong and an expensive tool ends up producing a daily report nobody reads.

## 1. Find the repetitive work
Write down what your marketing team actually did for one week. A surprising share of time usually goes to things like:

- Copying results from each ad platform into a spreadsheet
- Rewriting the same product's ad copy to fit each channel's format
- Routing incoming inquiries to the right person

Of these, work that has **clear rules, repeats often and can be undone if it goes wrong** is your first automation candidate.

## 2. Set the metrics first
"AI will make us more efficient" is not a metric. Decide the numbers you'll compare before and after.

- Hours spent writing reports each week
- Time to produce one ad creative
- Time for an inquiry to reach the right person

## 3. Keep an approval step
AI-written copy and reports are drafts. Build a human check into the process for brand tone, ad review rules and facts. In finance and virtual assets especially, a single phrase can cause trouble, so the review step is essential.

> The goal of automation isn't to remove people. It's to give them more time for judgment.
